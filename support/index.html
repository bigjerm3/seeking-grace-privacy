Daily Verse & Worship

Seeking Grace is a daily worship and verse app for iPhone. It works offline. There is no account. This page answers common questions and how to reach us.

Daily watch and the bell

Set the watch hour in Settings. If you allow notifications and alarms, Seeking Grace can knock at that hour until the day’s worship and verse are kept. Opening the app mutes the bell; leaving before Amen may start it again. You can change or silence this in Settings inside the app, or in iOS Settings.

The hedge

The hedge uses Apple Screen Time to hold apps you name from the watch hour until Amen. If the hedge will not arm, check Screen Time permission for Seeking Grace in iOS Settings. Seeking Grace does not see the names of your other apps beyond the opaque tokens you choose.

Keep (subscription)

Keep is sold through the App Store. Prices and free trials come from Apple. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, or from Keep inside the app. Restoring purchases uses your Apple ID on this device.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy for what stays on your device and how Firebase Analytics is used.

Contact

For help with Seeking Grace, reply through the App Store listing for Seeking Grace, or contact the developer account that publishes the app. Include your device model and iOS version when you write about a problem.
