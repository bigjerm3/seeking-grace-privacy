Seeking Grace

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Seeking Grace is a daily worship and verse app for iPhone. It works offline. There is no account. This page explains what the app does with information on your device.

What we collect

Seeking Grace does not collect, sell, or share personal information. We do not run analytics, advertising, or tracking. The app does not send your worship, verse progress, prayer words, streak, player names, or settings to a server.

What stays on your device

The following stay on your iPhone (and, where needed, in the app’s shared group on that device):

  • Days you keep, your streak, and today’s completion
  • Watch hour, bell, hedge, and prayer by the water
  • Player names for the house quiz, and path progress
  • Apps you name for the hedge (as Apple Screen Time tokens)
  • Whether Seeking Grace Keep is active on this Apple ID

Notifications and alarms

If you allow notifications and alarms, Seeking Grace schedules them on your device for the daily watch. Opening the app can mute the bell. Leaving before Amen may schedule a further knock. These stay under your control in iOS Settings.

Screen Time (the hedge)

If you turn on the hedge, Apple’s Family Controls / Screen Time APIs hold the apps you name from the watch hour until Amen. Seeking Grace does not receive a list of app names from Apple beyond the opaque tokens you choose on your device.

Subscriptions (Keep)

Purchases are handled by Apple. Seeking Grace uses StoreKit to check whether Keep is active. Payment details are managed by Apple, not by Seeking Grace. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Children

Seeking Grace does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The daily practice is suitable for family use on a shared device under a parent’s care.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy for Seeking Grace may be sent through the App Store listing for Seeking Grace, or to the developer account that publishes the app.
